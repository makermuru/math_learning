750 metres is 750 out of 1,000 metres. That is 0.75 of a kilometre.

If 1,000 metres makes one whole kilometre, then 750 metres is most of that kilometre, but not all of it.

Decimals show parts of a whole

Think of 1 whole kilometre as a strip split into 100 equal parts.

0.75
Seven tenths and five hundredths · seventy-five hundredths

Look at the hundred-square

Each tiny square is 0.01. 75 shaded squares make 0.75. The same amount is 750 metres out of 1,000 metres.
